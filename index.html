The Myth of the Aurora 2

After years of being unheard of, the wreck of the Aurora 2 spaceship came up on a radar. You have been sent to read through all remaining logs, journals, and files of the Aurora 2. Your assignment is to piece together what happened to the Aurora 2 and the fates of the 4 crewmembers that were onboard. You can choose to conclude your investiagation whenever you are satisifed with your conclusion.
